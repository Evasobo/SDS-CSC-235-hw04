Temperature Uncertainty Viewer

Important: This visualization uses synthetic weather data generated using a Python-based simulation (Faker library). Values reflect realistic seasonal patterns but are not real-world measurements.

This visualization shows average daily February temperatures for multiple cities. The shaded blue band represents a simulated uncertainty range based on possible temperature variation throughout the day. Users can compare trends, variability, and average temperatures between cities.

Average Temperature by City

Bars represent average simulated February temperatures by city. Black error bars show 95% confidence intervals, representing uncertainty in the estimated mean temperature.
